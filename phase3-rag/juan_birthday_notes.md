## Ceremony: Baptism

The baptism ceremony is at 11:00 hs at Parroquia San Ignacio de Loyola,
located at Bolívar 225, in the Monserrat neighborhood of Buenos Aires, right
next to Plaza de Mayo. It's one of the oldest churches in the city, part of
the Manzana de las Luces complex.

**Getting there from Quilmes:** the most straightforward option is the Roca
train line — take it from Quilmes station toward Plaza Constitución (roughly
30–40 minutes depending on the service). From Constitución, it's about a
10–15 minute trip by taxi/remis or Subte Línea C (Constitución–Retiro) to the
Avenida de Mayo station, followed by a short walk to the church. Driving from
Quilmes, the general route is via the Autopista Buenos Aires–La Plata toward
downtown, then local streets to Bolívar and Perú/Alsina — expect 30–45
minutes depending on traffic, more on a Saturday if there's an event around
Plaza de Mayo.

**Parking near the church:** this is downtown Monserrat, right by Plaza de
Mayo, so parking is mostly metered on-street parking ("estacionamiento
medido") plus a handful of commercial parking garages along Av. de Mayo and
the surrounding streets. Because this is a busy, central area, it's worth
checking Google Maps or a parking app shortly before the 21st for the closest
available option rather than relying on this note — spots and garage
availability in this area change.

## Party: Molina Ciudad

The party will be at 13hs, at the Molina Ciudad building, Av. Regimiento de Patricios
1052, on the Barracas/La Boca border — the building where Matías and Melisa
used to live two years ago. 

**Getting there from the church:** it's about 4 km south of Bolívar 225.
Driving, the general route is via Av. Paseo Colón and then Av. Almirante
Brown or Av. Martín García toward Av. Regimiento de Patricios — roughly
10–15 minutes by car outside of heavy traffic. By public transport, several
colectivo lines connect the Constitución/Barracas corridor to this stretch
of Av. Regimiento de Patricios — it's worth checking Moovit or Google Maps
closer to the day for the current best line and stop, since exact routes can
shift.

**Parking near Molina Ciudad:** Barracas is less congested than downtown
Monserrat, and street parking tends to be easier to find near the building,
though as with the church, it's worth checking on arrival rather than
assuming a specific spot will be free.
